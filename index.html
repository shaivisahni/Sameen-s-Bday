The guest list

Six turtles and tortoises who RSVP'd yes. Each one brought a party trick.

Ocean traveller

Green sea turtle

Chelonia mydas

Females often return to the very beach where they hatched to lay their own eggs, sometimes decades later.

Deep diver

Leatherback

Dermochelys coriacea

No hard shell at all. Oily, leathery skin covers its back, and it can dive deeper than 1,000 metres.

Island giant

Galápagos tortoise

Chelonoidis niger complex

Shell shape depends on the island. Tortoises from dry islands have saddle-shaped shells that let them reach higher plants.

Shell hinge

Eastern box turtle

Terrapene carolina

The hinged underside closes up tight, so the whole turtle becomes a shut box when something scary walks by.

Red cheeks

Red-eared slider

Trachemys scripta elegans

Named for the red stripe behind each eye. It is the most common pet turtle in the world, and a well-known invader far from home.

Sneaky host

Alligator snapping turtle

Macrochelys temminckii

Waits with its mouth open and wiggles a pink, worm-shaped tongue to lure fish straight in.
